# Code of conduct

MoMo Lens contributors should be able to participate without harassment or discrimination.

Be respectful, explain disagreements with evidence, welcome different levels of experience, and protect other people's privacy. Give useful feedback about the work. Do not publish personal information, use threats or sexual harassment, make discriminatory attacks, or disrupt discussions repeatedly.

These expectations apply in project issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, ask someone to change their behavior, temporarily restrict participation, or ban repeated or serious misconduct. They should explain a decision privately where practical and consider context, severity, and prior behavior.

Report conduct problems through a private maintainer contact listed in the repository's current profile. If no private channel is available, ask a maintainer to provide one without publishing the incident or the people involved. Maintainers must publish a suitable conduct contact before a public community launch. Never include a private statement as evidence.
